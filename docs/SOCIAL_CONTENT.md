# MECGURA: Instagram carousel aur reel scripts (Hinglish)

Har industry ke liye: 6-slide carousel, 30 second ka reel script, aur caption. Sab example hain. **Asli client ka naam ya result tabhi daalein jab sach ho aur client ne ijazat di ho.**

## Posting rules
- Hafte mein 3 reels aur 2 carousels.
- Har post ke last mein ek hi CTA: 'Free demo, link bio mein' (https://mecgura.tech/demo).
- Ads ya offer wale post mein jhoothe claims na likhein.
- Reels mein screen recording rakhein: apne doosre phone se bot ka reply dikhayein. Wahi sabse convincing hota hai.
