# MECGURA WhatsApp templates (agency pack)

One click: **Industry packs → Digital marketing agency (MECGURA) → Apply**. It adds the templates below as drafts (nothing is sent to Meta until you press Submit), five tags and a paused AI assistant.

Marketing templates carry the footer "Reply STOP to unsubscribe". Language is English (Roman letters).

## Rules to follow
- Marketing templates go only to people who agreed to get messages (opt-in).
- Utility templates only for something the customer did (demo booked, invoice, payment, work update). Meta may move a template to Marketing if it reads like a promotion; feedback_request is the one most likely to be moved.
- If Meta rejects one, open it in Templates: the template doctor explains why and makes a fixed copy.
