# Website photo credits

All photos on mecgura.tech come from free stock libraries (Pexels first, Unsplash second) under their free
commercial-use licences. No paid or subscription images (Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, Getty) are used.

Rules:

- Store optimised WebP copies in `public/images/mecgura/` with descriptive names; never hotlink.
- Check each photo's own page and licence before use, and record it below.
- Stock models are never presented as MECGURA customers or testimonials; the home page says the businesses and
  chats are examples.
