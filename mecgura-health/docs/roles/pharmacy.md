# Pharmacy staff / manager — quick guide

Dispenses medicines and keeps stock correct.

## What you do here
1. **Dispensing**: pick a finalised prescription, dispense batch-wise (nearest expiry first); partial dispensing is supported.
2. **Stock**: batches, expiry alerts, low-stock alerts, adjustments (reason required), returns.
3. **Purchases & suppliers** (Manager): record purchases, receive stock, approve returns.
4. **Reports** (Manager): stock valuation, expiry, movement.

## Good to know
- Stock never goes negative — the system blocks it.
- Expired or blocked batches cannot be dispensed.
- Every adjustment needs a reason and is audited.

Need help? Ask your Clinic Admin; they can contact MECGURA support with the request id shown on any error.
