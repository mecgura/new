# Accountant — quick guide

Finance: invoices, payments, refunds, reports.

## What you do here
1. **Billing**: invoices, payments, outstanding balances.
2. **Refunds**: process approved refunds; every step is audited.
3. **Reports**: collections, outstanding, doctor/service revenue; CSV export (audited).
4. **Analytics**: financial dashboards.

## Good to know
- Exports contain money data — keep them secure.
- Payment status is never marked paid without a recorded payment or a verified provider event.

Need help? Ask your Clinic Admin; they can contact MECGURA support with the request id shown on any error.
