# Doctor — quick guide

Consults patients, writes prescriptions, orders tests, manages follow-ups.

## What you do here
1. **OPD**: call the next patient; start the consultation from the visit.
2. **Consultation**: vitals, history, diagnosis, notes — saved as versions; finalise to lock the prescription.
3. **Prescription**: add medicines, finalise, print/download.
4. **Lab orders**: order investigations; review released reports.
5. **Follow-ups**: set the next visit/recall; the system reminds the patient only through configured channels.
6. **Patients**: view clinical history (your clinic only).
7. **Analytics**: your own consultation numbers.

## Good to know
- The product does not suggest diagnoses or treatments; clinical decisions are yours.
- Finalised prescriptions cannot be silently edited — amendments create a new version.
- Do not share your login.

Need help? Ask your Clinic Admin; they can contact MECGURA support with the request id shown on any error.
