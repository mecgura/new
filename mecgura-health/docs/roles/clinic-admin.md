# Clinic Admin — quick guide

Owns the clinic's setup, people and settings; sees everything inside the clinic.

## What you do here
1. **Team**: add staff (invitation), assign roles, extra permissions, deactivate leavers.
2. **Settings**: clinic profile, branding (logo, colours), scheduling rules, billing settings, communication templates, lab and follow-up settings, privacy and security (require two-factor for roles).
3. **Website**: edit and publish the public site; review enquiries.
4. **Subscription**: plan, invoices, payment status.
5. **Privacy**: handle patient data-rights requests within 30 days; review consent.
6. **Integrations**: see which channels are configured (read-only status).

## Good to know
- Turn on two-factor for doctors, accountants and admins.
- Review the team list monthly and remove people who left.
- Refunds and discounts follow the approval roles you set.

Need help? Ask your Clinic Admin; they can contact MECGURA support with the request id shown on any error.
