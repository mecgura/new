# Patient (portal) — quick guide

Sees their own records at their clinic. Clinic staff cannot see your password.

## What you do here
1. Sign in at your clinic's portal address (your clinic gives you the link or code).
2. **Appointments**: book, reschedule, cancel when the clinic allows it.
3. **Records**: consultations, prescriptions, lab reports, invoices and payments — only yours.
4. **Requests**: ask the clinic for corrections, a copy of your data, or to restrict processing; the clinic has 30 days to respond.
5. **Privacy & security**: consents, notification preferences, change password, log out everywhere.

## Good to know
- Never share your password.
- The portal shows records the clinic has released; it does not give medical advice.
- If you do not receive messages, the clinic may not have that channel switched on.

Need help? Ask your Clinic Admin; they can contact MECGURA support with the request id shown on any error.
