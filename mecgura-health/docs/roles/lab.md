# Lab Staff — quick guide

Collects samples and enters results.

## What you do here
1. **Lab**: pending orders → collect sample (printable label; no barcode scanner support) → enter results → submit for review.
2. **Rejections**: reject a sample with a reason; recollection creates a new sample and the old one stays on record.
3. **Reports**: released reports can be printed; amendments are versioned.
4. Review/release requires the separate review permission.

## Good to know
- Never edit a released report — request an amendment.
- Reference ranges come from the lab settings configured by the Clinic Admin.

Need help? Ask your Clinic Admin; they can contact MECGURA support with the request id shown on any error.
