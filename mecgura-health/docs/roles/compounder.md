# Compounder / Nurse assistant — quick guide

Supports the doctor in the room: queue and vitals.

## What you do here
1. **OPD queue**: see who is next, mark ready.
2. **Vitals**: record weight, BP, pulse, temperature etc. on the consultation.
3. **Prescriptions**: view/print finalised ones for the patient.

## Good to know
- You cannot finalise prescriptions or see billing.
- Record only what was measured.

Need help? Ask your Clinic Admin; they can contact MECGURA support with the request id shown on any error.
